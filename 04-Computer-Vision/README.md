# 04 - Computer Vision

> Visual perception, image understanding, object detection, pixel-level segmentation, 3D reconstruction, and generative restoration.

## 🌐 Overview & Architectural Importance

Computer vision empowers machines to perceive, parse, and reason about the physical visual world. From autonomous vehicles and surgical robotics to foundation vision-language models (VLMs) and neural radiance fields, visual intelligence is central to embodied and multimodal artificial intelligence.

## 📑 Curated Learning Guides

Explore in-depth roadmaps, authoritative textbooks, official documentation, courses, and implementations for each subtopic:

* [**Core Vision**](Core-Vision.md) — Classical image processing, OpenCV, filtering, edge detection, color spaces, and visual transformations.
* [**Object Detection**](Object-Detection.md) — YOLO family (v8, v11), Faster R-CNN, DETR transformers, and real-time bounding box regression.
* [**Segmentation**](Segmentation.md) — Semantic, instance, and panoptic segmentation, including Segment Anything (SAM/SAM 2) and Mask R-CNN.
* [**OCR**](OCR.md) — Optical character recognition, text detection, and document text parsing with PaddleOCR and Tesseract.
* [**Pose Estimation**](Pose-Estimation.md) — 2D and 3D human body, hand, and facial landmark tracking with MediaPipe and OpenPose.
* [**Face Analysis**](Face-Analysis.md) — Facial recognition, embedding verification, attribute detection, and anti-spoofing with InsightFace and DeepFace.
* [**Video Understanding**](Video-Understanding.md) — Action recognition, spatiotemporal video transformers, tracking, and optical flow.
* [**3D Vision**](3D-Vision.md) — 3D point clouds, NeRF (Neural Radiance Fields), 3D Gaussian Splatting, and mesh geometry with PyTorch3D.
* [**Image Restoration & Enhancement**](Image-Restoration-and-Enhancement.md) — Super-resolution, denoising, deblurring, and face restoration with Real-ESRGAN and GFPGAN.

## 🧭 Recommended Study Sequence

1. **Classical Image Fundamentals**: Pixel operations, convolutions, kernel filtering, and OpenCV transformations.
2. **Deep Vision Backbone**: CNN architectures (ResNet, ConvNeXt) and Vision Transformers (ViT).
3. **Detection & Localization**: Bounding box regression, anchor boxes, NMS, IoU, and real-time YOLO pipelines.
4. **Pixel-Level Segmentation**: Mask R-CNN, U-Net, and foundation zero-shot segmentation with Meta's SAM 2.
5. **Spatial & 3D Intelligence**: 3D point clouds, camera calibration, NeRFs, and 3D Gaussian Splatting.

## 🗝️ Foundational Prerequisites

- Convolution arithmetic: kernel sizes, stride, padding, dilation, and receptive fields.
- PyTorch tensor manipulations and basic neural network training loops.
- Coordinate geometries, transformations, and bounding box formats (xyxy vs xywh).
