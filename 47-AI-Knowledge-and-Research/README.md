# 47 - AI Knowledge & Research

> Scientific discovery engines, automated literature review systems, citation graph explorers, and deep research agents that accelerate scientific inquiry.

## 🌐 Overview & Architectural Importance

With over 100,000 AI and STEM research papers published annually, human researchers face severe information overload. AI research tools, citation graph navigators, and autonomous research agents (like STORM and PaperQA) synthesize evidence, uncover hidden connections, and automate literature reviews.

## 📑 Curated Learning Guides

Explore in-depth roadmaps, authoritative textbooks, official documentation, courses, and implementations for each subtopic:

* [**Research Tools**](Research-Tools.md) — Semantic Scholar, OpenAlex, Papers With Code, Connected Papers, and research discovery platforms.
* [**Research Assistants**](Research-Assistants.md) — Stanford STORM, PaperQA, Elicit, ScholarAI, and multi-agent synthesis engines.
* [**Literature Review**](Literature-Review.md) — Litmaps, Research Rabbit, citation network mapping, and systematic literature review pipelines.

## 🧭 Recommended Study Sequence

1. **Academic Graph APIs**: Accessing OpenAlex and Semantic Scholar graph databases via Python.
2. **Citation Mapping**: Discovering seminal ancestors and derivative papers with Connected Papers and Litmaps.
3. **Grounded QA on Research Papers**: Using PaperQA to answer technical questions with exact verbatim citations.
4. **Autonomous Research Agents**: Stanford STORM generating Wikipedia-style referenced technical reports.
5. **Synthesizing Meta-Analyses**: Automating PRISMA literature reviews with AI pipelines.

## 🗝️ Foundational Prerequisites

- Bibliometric networks, co-citation analysis, and PageRank.
- Information retrieval, vector search, and citation parsing.
- Academic reading strategies (pass 1: title/abstract -> pass 2: figures/conclusions -> pass 3: math/proofs).
