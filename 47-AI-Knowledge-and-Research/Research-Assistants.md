# Research Assistants

> Autonomous multi-agent systems and retrieval engines that synthesize scientific literature, answer complex queries, and draft referenced literature reviews.

## 🎯 What to Learn

- Stanford STORM: Multi-agent system that searches the web, conducts perspective interviews, and generates full Wikipedia-style referenced reports
- PaperQA: Question answering over scientific PDFs with exact paragraph-level citations and hallucination penalties
- Elicit: Autonomous research assistant finding claims, methodology details, and effect sizes across 200M papers
- Multi-agent perspective-driven inquiry: Simulating expert conversations to uncover niche viewpoints
- Citation grounding: Enforcing strict verbatim evidence constraints to eliminate hallucinations in scientific writing
- Exporting research syntheses to LaTeX, Markdown, and BibTeX formats

## 🧭 Recommended Learning Path

1. Understand why naive RAG fails on broad scientific questions (lack of perspective diversity, cherry-picking).
2. Study Stanford STORM's two-phase architecture: Pre-writing (discovery interviews) -> Writing (outline generation and polish).
3. Run PaperQA locally on a collection of PDFs to experience evidence-grounded QA.
4. Explore Elicit for automated systematic literature table extraction.
5. Integrate automated reference management with Zotero and BibTeX.
