# Literature Review

> Visual exploration platforms, citation mapping networks, and systematic review tools that trace the chronological and conceptual evolution of scientific fields.

## 🎯 What to Learn

- Citation network topology: Direct citations, co-citations, bibliographic coupling, and citation velocity
- Litmaps: Interactive visualization creating chronological citation timelines and discovering missing literature
- Research Rabbit: Collaborative literature exploration tool that learns user interests and recommends connected papers
- PRISMA guidelines (Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic Reviews and Meta-Analyses)
- Automated extraction of methodology, sample size, effect size, and confidence intervals
- Building reproducible literature databases and tracking emerging research trends

## 🧭 Recommended Learning Path

1. Understand the difference between a narrative literature review and a systematic literature review.
2. Input a seminal paper into Litmaps to generate a chronological citation timeline.
3. Use Research Rabbit to discover serendipitous connections through author and institution clusters.
4. Define explicit inclusion and exclusion criteria following PRISMA guidelines.
5. Export structured synthesis matrices into your reference manager (Zotero/Mendeley).
