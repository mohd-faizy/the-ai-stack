# 49 - Bioinformatics

> Computational biology, genomic sequence analysis, single-cell transcriptomics, and structural biology software powering precision medicine.

## 🌐 Overview & Architectural Importance

The biological code of life—DNA, RNA, and proteins—is the ultimate digital sequence. Modern bioinformatics applies transformer language models (DNABERT, Enformer), variational autoencoders (scvi-tools), and deep structural predictors to decode disease mechanisms and customize therapies.

## 📑 Curated Learning Guides

Explore in-depth roadmaps, authoritative textbooks, official documentation, courses, and implementations for each subtopic:

* [**Genomics**](Genomics.md) — Biopython, DeepVariant, Enformer, DNABERT, and genomic foundation models.
* [**Single Cell Analysis**](Single-Cell-Analysis.md) — Scanpy, scvi-tools, CellTypist, AnnData, and single-cell RNA sequencing.
* [**Structural Biology**](Structural-Biology.md) — AlphaFold, OpenFold, ESMFold, Chai-1, and macromolecular complex prediction.

## 🧭 Recommended Study Sequence

1. **Biological Sequence Analysis**: Fast sequence manipulation with Biopython and nucleotide k-mers.
2. **Variant Calling**: DeepVariant convolutional networks accurately identifying SNPs and indels.
3. **Genomic Transformers**: Modeling distal gene regulation (100kb+ context) with Enformer.
4. **Single-Cell Transcriptomics**: Clustering, cell-type annotation, and trajectory inference with Scanpy and scvi-tools.
5. **Multimeric Structural Biology**: Modeling protein-protein, protein-DNA, and protein-RNA complexes.

## 🗝️ Foundational Prerequisites

- Molecular biology fundamentals (Central Dogma: DNA -> RNA -> Protein).
- High-throughput sequencing technologies (Illumina NGS, PacBio long reads, 10x Genomics).
- Python data analysis (NumPy, SciPy, AnnData, Biopython).
