# 54 - AI for Climate & Environment

> Planetary-scale satellite analytics, environmental monitoring engines, carbon tracking software, and climate foundation models.

## 🌐 Overview & Architectural Importance

Tackling global climate change requires continuous Earth observation, renewable energy forecasting, and deforestation monitoring across petabytes of geospatial imagery. AI models (TorchGeo, Microsoft Planetary Computer, SatCLIP) enable real-time environmental monitoring while tools like CodeCarbon ensure AI's own carbon footprint is quantified and minimized.

## 🧭 Recommended Study Sequence

1. **Geospatial Deep Learning**: Multiband satellite imagery (Sentinel-2, Landsat) and TorchGeo data loaders.
2. **Planetary Computer APIs**: Accessing petabytes of cloud-native STAC (SpatioTemporal Asset Catalog) imagery.
3. **Global Climate Emulators**: Microsoft Aurora and ClimaX modeling long-term planetary atmospheric variables.
4. **Earth Observation Foundation Models**: Prithvi and SatCLIP for zero-shot land cover and crop classification.
5. **Green AI Engineering**: Measuring real-time GPU wattages and carbon intensity with CodeCarbon.

## 🗝️ Foundational Prerequisites

- Geospatial data concepts: Coordinate reference systems (CRS), multispectral bands, and GeoTIFFs.
- Earth observation data formats (STAC, Zarr, NetCDF).
- Computer vision architectures: Semantic segmentation, UNet, and Vision Transformers.
