# Climate Models

> Atmospheric foundation models, multi-scale Earth system emulators, and long-range climate projection systems powered by deep learning.

## 🎯 What to Learn

- Difference between weather forecasting (1-14 days) and climate projection (decades to centuries)
- Microsoft Aurora: High-resolution foundation model for the atmosphere predicting air pollution and climate variables
- Microsoft ClimaX: The first foundation model for weather and climate trained on diverse heterogeneous climate datasets
- Downscaling: Using super-resolution neural networks to convert coarse 100km global climate models to local 1km projections
- Emulating physical climate models (CMIP6): Replacing expensive general circulation models with fast neural emulators
- Tipping points and extreme event attribution: Modeling heatwaves, droughts, and sea surface temperature anomalies

## 🧭 Recommended Learning Path

1. Understand the difference between weather forecasting and climate simulation.
2. Explore NetCDF and Zarr file formats for multi-dimensional spatiotemporal climate arrays.
3. Study climate downscaling and super-resolution architectures.
4. Explore Microsoft ClimaX and Aurora foundation models.
5. Participate in Climate Change AI webinars and community working groups.
