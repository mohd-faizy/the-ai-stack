# Risk Analysis

> Portfolio optimization frameworks, performance analytics, and statistical risk metrics to measure drawdowns, downside risk, and asset allocation.

## 🎯 What to Learn

- Core risk metrics: Sharpe Ratio, Sortino Ratio, Maximum Drawdown, Value at Risk (VaR), and Conditional VaR (CVaR)
- Riskfolio-Lib: Quantitative portfolio optimization library in Python supporting Modern Portfolio Theory and hierarchical risk parity
- QuantStats: Portfolio analytics library generating detailed tear-sheets, rolling beta, and performance visualizations
- Hierarchical Risk Parity (HRP): Clustering covariance matrices to allocate capital without inverting unstable matrices
- Black-Litterman model: Combining investor market equilibrium views with historical covariances
- Stress testing and Monte Carlo historical simulations of market shocks

## 🧭 Recommended Learning Path

1. Understand Modern Portfolio Theory: Markowitz efficient frontier and covariance matrices.
2. Calculate key performance ratios: Sharpe Ratio, Sortino Ratio, and Calmar Ratio with QuantStats.
3. Compute Value at Risk (VaR) and Expected Shortfall (CVaR) at the 99% confidence level.
4. Learn Hierarchical Risk Parity (HRP) to overcome the instability of matrix inversion.
5. Generate automated risk tear-sheets to communicate performance to stakeholders.
