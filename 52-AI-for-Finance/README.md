# 52 - AI for Finance

> Algorithmic trading engines, quantitative finance libraries, financial NLP models, real-time fraud detection, and portfolio risk management systems.

## 🌐 Overview & Architectural Importance

Financial markets generate high-velocity, noisy, non-stationary time series data. Applying reinforcement learning (FinRL), specialized financial language models (FinBERT), and anomaly detection to market microstructure and risk analytics drives modern quantitative trading.

## 📑 Curated Learning Guides

Explore in-depth roadmaps, authoritative textbooks, official documentation, courses, and implementations for each subtopic:

* [**Quantitative Finance**](Quantitative-Finance.md) — FinRL, Qlib, FinGPT, Zipline, Backtrader, vectorbt, and algorithmic trading.
* [**Financial NLP**](Financial-NLP.md) — FinBERT, BloombergGPT, 10-K report parsing, and market sentiment extraction.
* [**Fraud Detection**](Fraud-Detection.md) — PyOD, Alibi Detect, credit card fraud detection, and unsupervised anomaly detection.
* [**Risk Analysis**](Risk-Analysis.md) — Riskfolio-Lib, QuantStats, Value at Risk (VaR), Sharpe ratios, and portfolio optimization.

## 🧭 Recommended Study Sequence

1. **Market Data & Backtesting**: Vectorized backtesting with vectorbt and event-driven backtesting with Backtrader.
2. **Quantitative Machine Learning**: Microsoft Qlib for automated factor mining and signal generation.
3. **Reinforcement Learning in Trading**: Multi-asset trading environments with FinRL.
4. **Financial Sentiment & Disclosures**: Parsing SEC EDGAR 10-K filings with FinBERT.
5. **Portfolio Risk & Optimization**: Modern Portfolio Theory and Value at Risk with Riskfolio-Lib.

## 🗝️ Foundational Prerequisites

- Financial market mechanics (order books, bid-ask spread, liquidity, slippage).
- Time-series econometrics (stationarity, cointegration, ARCH/GARCH).
- Optimization theory (convex optimization, quadratic programming).
