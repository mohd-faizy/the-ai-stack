# Quantitative Finance

> Algorithmic trading frameworks, reinforcement learning market environments, and AI-driven quantitative investment strategies.

## 🎯 What to Learn

- Backtesting paradigms: Vectorized backtesting (vectorbt) vs event-driven backtesting (Zipline, Backtrader)
- Microsoft Qlib: AI-oriented quantitative investment platform for automated alpha factor mining and model management
- FinRL (Columbia University & AI4Finance): Open-source framework for financial reinforcement learning across stock and crypto trading
- Financial data feeds: Alpha Vantage, Yahoo Finance, Polygon.io, and Level 2 order book data
- Market microstructure: Order book dynamics, bid-ask spread, market impact, and slippage modeling
- Avoiding lookahead bias and survivorship bias in quantitative backtests

## 🧭 Recommended Learning Path

1. Understand financial time series: OHLCV bars, returns, and stationarity tests (ADF test).
2. Learn vectorized backtesting with vectorbt for ultra-fast signal exploration.
3. Explore event-driven execution and simulate slippage and commissions in Backtrader.
4. Train a deep reinforcement learning agent using FinRL on historical price data.
5. Study Marcos Lopez de Prado's methodologies to prevent backtest overfitting.
