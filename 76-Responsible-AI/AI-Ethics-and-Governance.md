# AI Ethics & Governance

> AI Ethics and Governance encompasses the regulatory frameworks, organizational policies, model documentation protocols, and impact assessment tools that ensure AI systems operate lawfully, safely, and in alignment with societal values.

## 🎯 What to Learn

- Model documentation standards: Model Cards, System Cards, and Datasheets for Datasets
- Regulatory landscape: EU AI Act (risk tiers), US Executive Order on AI, and NIST AI RMF
- AI transparency, auditability, and provenance standards (C2PA)
- Red-teaming protocols and dual-use risk assessments
- Organizational governance workflows and Responsible AI committees

- [Model Cards for Model Reporting](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.03993) — Mitchell et al. (Google Research) seminal proposal for standardized documentation of machine learning models.
- [Datasheets for Datasets](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.09010) — Gebru et al. fundamental framework for documenting dataset provenance, composition, and collection process.

### 🧪 Practice

## 🧭 Recommended Learning Path

1. Read the seminal 'Model Cards for Model Reporting' and 'Datasheets for Datasets' papers.
2. Study the NIST AI Risk Management Framework (Map, Measure, Manage, Govern).
3. Analyze the risk tiers and compliance mandates of the EU AI Act.
4. Create a production-grade Model Card for an open-source classifier or generative model.
5. Implement automated ethical red-teaming checks before deploying foundation models.
