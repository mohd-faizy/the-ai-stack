# 76 - Responsible AI

> Responsible AI encompasses frameworks, audit tools, and methodologies designed to build artificial intelligence systems that are fair, transparent, privacy-preserving, ethical, and accountable to society. As AI models are increasingly deployed in high-stakes domains like criminal justice, hiring, lending, and medicine, understanding disparate impact, bias mitigation algorithms, and governance frameworks is vital for production AI engineering.
