# Generative AI Courses

> Open curricula, university sequences, and developer training courses covering multimodal generative AI, diffusion models, and creative applications.

## 🎯 What to Learn

- Generative modeling paradigms: GANs (Generative Adversarial Networks), VAEs, Autoregressive Models, and Diffusion Models
- Microsoft Generative AI for Beginners: 18-lesson curriculum exploring LLMs, embeddings, RAG, and app development
- Hugging Face Diffusion Models Class: Deep dive into the theory, mathematics, and PyTorch coding of diffusion pipelines
- DeepLearning.AI Generative AI with LLMs: Foundational course taught by AWS and DeepLearning.AI experts
- Multimodal generation: Text-to-Image, Text-to-Audio, and Video synthesis architectures
- Ethical considerations, watermarking, copyright, and safety filters

## 🧭 Recommended Learning Path

1. Understand probability density estimation and generative vs discriminative models.
2. Work through Microsoft Generative AI for Beginners.
3. Complete the Hugging Face Diffusion Models Class to understand image synthesis.
4. Read David Foster's Generative Deep Learning book.
5. Build a multimodal generative application combining vision and audio.
