# LAION

> Large-scale Artificial Intelligence Open Network: Non-profit collective creating massive open multimodal datasets (LAION-5B).

## 🎯 What to Learn

- The LAION mission: Democratizing access to large-scale multimodal training datasets for researchers globally
- LAION-5B: The seminal 5.85 billion image-text pair dataset that enabled the training of Stable Diffusion and OpenCLIP
- Dataset filtering mechanics: CLIP-based image-text cosine similarity thresholding, aesthetic scoring, and watermark filtering
- OpenCLIP: Open-source reproduction of OpenAI's CLIP model trained on open LAION datasets matching official benchmarks
- Data compliance, safety filters, and ethical curation of public web scrapes
- Distributed scraping and tokenization using Common Crawl WARC files
