# EleutherAI

> EleutherAI: Grassroots open-science collective, creators of GPT-NeoX, Pythia, The Pile, and the LM Evaluation Harness.

## 🎯 What to Learn

- Open science mission: Decentralizing AI research and democratizing frontier model training for the global community
- EleutherAI LM Evaluation Harness: The universal industry standard evaluation framework used by Hugging Face Leaderboard
- GPT-Neo & GPT-NeoX: Early landmark open reproductions of GPT-3 architecture capable of scaling across GPU clusters
- The Pythia model suite: 16 models trained across identical data order, enabling scientific study of memorization and training dynamics
- The Pile: 825GB open-source diverse language modeling dataset that laid the groundwork for open training data
- Interpretability research: Probing transformer internal circuits and training dynamics
