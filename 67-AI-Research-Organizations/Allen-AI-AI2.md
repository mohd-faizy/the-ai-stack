# Allen AI (AI2)

> Allen Institute for AI (AI2): Champions of truly open foundation models, creators of OLMo, Dolma, and Semantic Scholar.

## 🎯 What to Learn

- Truly open AI: Releasing not just model weights, but complete training code, exact data recipes, training logs, and intermediate checkpoints
- OLMo (Open Language Model): State-of-the-art open models with full transparency across data, training, and evaluation
- Dolma: 3-trillion token open dataset with complete open-source curation and filtering tooling
- Semantic Scholar: AI-powered academic search engine indexing hundreds of millions of scientific papers
- AI2 TÜLU: Open instruction-tuning and alignment suite matching proprietary alignment quality
- Scientific NLP: SciBERT, SciSpacy, and building AI for scientific understanding
