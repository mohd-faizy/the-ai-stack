# Stanford University

> Stanford CRFM and Stanford AI Lab: Academic creators of HELM, Alpaca, DSPy, and foundational NLP curricula.

## 🎯 What to Learn

- Stanford CRFM (Center for Research on Foundation Models): Academic leadership, ethical audits, and holistic evaluation (HELM)
- Stanford Alpaca: Pioneering self-instruct distillation, fine-tuning Llama 7B for under $500 and launching the open instruction-tuning movement
- DSPy (Omar Khattab et al.): Programming framework that treats prompting as compiler optimization rather than brittle prompt hacking
- Stanford NLP Group: CoreNLP, GloVe embeddings, and seminal computational linguistics research
- World-class open courses: CS224N (NLP with Deep Learning), CS231n (Computer Vision), and CS25 (Transformers United)
- Academic rigor: Investigating foundation model transparency, copyright, and societal risks

## 📚 Best Learning Resources
