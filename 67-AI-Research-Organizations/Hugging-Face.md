# Hugging Face

> Hugging Face: The community town square of machine learning, creators of Transformers, Datasets, TRL, Diffusers, and leaders in open science.

## 🎯 What to Learn

- The Hugging Face Hub: The world's largest repository of open-weights models (1M+), datasets (200k+), and interactive Spaces
- Transformers library: The universal standard Python library providing unified APIs for PyTorch, TensorFlow, and JAX models
- TRL (Transformer Reinforcement Learning): The go-to toolkit for post-training (SFT, DPO, PPO, GRPO)
- Diffusers: State-of-the-art toolbox for pretrained diffusion models across image, audio, and 3D generation
- Open Science Leadership: Open LLM Leaderboard, BigCode (StarCoder), and LeRobot open robotics
- Safetensors: Secure, zero-copy tensor serialization format replacing insecure pickle files
