# Microsoft Research

> Microsoft Research: Creators of ResNet, DeepSpeed, the Phi small language model family, AutoGen, and foundational systems infrastructure.

## 🎯 What to Learn

- Key scientific milestones: ResNet (Deep Residual Learning - He et al. 2015, the most cited paper in modern CS), Swin Transformer
- DeepSpeed: Extreme-scale distributed training optimization framework (ZeRO-1/2/3, ZeRO-Offload, MoE)
- Phi model family (Phi-1, Phi-2, Phi-3, Phi-4): Pioneering 'textbooks are all you need' small language models matching models 10x their size
- AutoGen & Semantic Kernel: Enterprise multi-agent conversation frameworks and AI application SDKs
- Graph RAG: Combining knowledge graphs with vector search for holistic query understanding
- ONNX Runtime: Cross-platform high-performance inference engine powering global enterprise AI
