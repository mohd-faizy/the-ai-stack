# NVIDIA

> NVIDIA and NVIDIA Research: The hardware and software bedrock of modern AI, creators of CUDA, TensorRT-LLM, Megatron-LM, and NeMo.

## 🎯 What to Learn

- Accelerated computing architecture: GPUs (Hopper H100, Blackwell B200), Tensor Cores, NVLink, and NVSwitch interconnects
- CUDA & cuDNN: The parallel computing platform and deep learning primitive library driving 99% of AI acceleration
- Megatron-LM: The industry standard framework for large-scale training using 3D tensor and pipeline parallelism
- TensorRT & TensorRT-LLM: Extreme-performance inference optimizers and runtimes for NVIDIA GPUs
- NVIDIA NeMo Framework: End-to-end platform for building, customizing, and deploying multimodal generative AI models
- Physical simulation and robotics: NVIDIA Isaac, Omniverse, and physics-informed Modulus
