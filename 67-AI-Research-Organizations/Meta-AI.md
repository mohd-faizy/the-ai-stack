# Meta AI (FAIR)

> Fundamental Artificial Intelligence Research (FAIR) and Meta Llama: Champions of open science, PyTorch, LLaMA, Segment Anything, and DINO.

## 🎯 What to Learn

- The open weights revolution: Meta Llama (Llama 1, 2, 3, 3.1, 3.2, 3.3) democratizing frontier AI for researchers and enterprises worldwide
- PyTorch: The open-source deep learning framework powering 90%+ of modern AI research and industrial deployment
- FAIR computer vision breakthroughs: Segment Anything (SAM 1 & 2), DINO / DINOv2, Detectron2, and Mask R-CNN
- Open science leadership: Yann LeCun's vision of open-weights foundation models, JEPA (Joint Embedding Predictive Architecture), and world models
- Audio and speech breakthroughs: SeamlessM4T, Voicebox, EnCodec, and AudioCraft
- Open infrastructure: Open Catalyst Project for renewable energy materials
