# Chinese AI Labs

> Frontier Chinese AI research organizations: DeepSeek, Alibaba DAMO (Qwen), Tsinghua THUDM (GLM), 01.AI (Yi), and Baidu.

## 🎯 What to Learn

- DeepSeek AI: Pioneer of DeepSeek-V3 (671B MoE with Multi-head Latent Attention) and DeepSeek-R1 (pure RL reasoning rivaling o1)
- Alibaba Cloud (Qwen): The leading global open-weights model family (Qwen2.5, Qwen2-VL, Qwen-Coder) dominating open leaderboards
- Tsinghua University (THUDM / Zhipu AI): ChatGLM, GLM-4, and visual foundation models
- 01.AI (Kai-Fu Lee): Yi model series setting high benchmarks in multilingual reasoning and long context
- Architectural innovations from Chinese labs: Multi-head Latent Attention (MLA), DualPipe parallel training, and low-cost MoE routing
- Extreme hardware efficiency: Training frontier models at a fraction of Western hyperscaler capital expenditure
