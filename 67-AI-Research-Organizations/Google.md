# Google & Google DeepMind

> Google Research and Google DeepMind: Pioneers of the Transformer architecture, AlphaFold, AlphaGo, Gemini, and open Gemma foundation models.

## 🎯 What to Learn

- Key scientific contributions: The Transformer architecture (Vaswani et al. 2017), BERT, T5, Word2Vec, and Sequence-to-Sequence learning
- Google DeepMind breakthroughs: AlphaGo, AlphaZero, AlphaFold2/3, GraphCast, and reinforcement learning foundations
- Gemini model family: Native multimodal foundation models processing text, code, audio, image, and video across 2M+ token contexts
- Gemma: Lightweight, state-of-the-art open models built from the same research and technology used to create Gemini
- Open-source developer ecosystems: JAX, TensorFlow, MediaPipe, DeepVariant, and Google AI Edge
- TPU (Tensor Processing Unit) hardware architecture and v5p/Trillium supercomputing pods
