# Statistics & Probability

> Probability and statistical inference provide the mathematical framework to quantify uncertainty, evaluate predictive risk, and model stochastic generative processes.

## 🎯 What to Learn

- Probability axioms, conditional probability, Independence, and Bayes' Rule
- Discrete & continuous random variables, PDFs, PMFs, and CDFs
- Distributions: Gaussian, Bernoulli, Binomial, Poisson, Beta, Dirichlet, and Student's t
- Expectation, variance, covariance, correlation, and mutual information
- Law of Large Numbers and Central Limit Theorem
- Maximum Likelihood Estimation (MLE) and Maximum A Posteriori (MAP)
- Hypothesis testing, p-values, confidence intervals, and bootstrapping
- Bayesian inference and Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC)

## 🧭 Recommended Learning Path

1. Master probability rules: conditional probability, independence, Bayes theorem.
2. Explore discrete and continuous probability distributions and their generative stories.
3. Understand sampling distributions, Central Limit Theorem, and standard errors.
4. Learn parameter estimation: Ordinary Least Squares (OLS) vs Maximum Likelihood (MLE) vs MAP.
5. Implement Bayesian updating and Markov Chain Monte Carlo with PyMC.
