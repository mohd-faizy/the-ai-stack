# 01 - Foundations

> The rigorous mathematical, statistical, optimization, and algorithmic underpinnings that power every modern artificial intelligence architecture.

## 🌐 Overview & Architectural Importance

Every machine learning algorithm—from basic linear regression to trillion-parameter reasoning foundation models—is an applied manifestation of linear algebra, multivariable calculus, probability theory, numerical optimization, and automatic differentiation. Mastery of these fundamentals is what distinguishes an AI engineer who truly understands model internals, stability, and loss dynamics from one who simply calls black-box APIs.

## 📑 Curated Learning Guides

Explore in-depth roadmaps, authoritative textbooks, official documentation, courses, and implementations for each subtopic:

* [**Mathematics & Linear Algebra**](Mathematics-and-Linear-Algebra.md) — Vector spaces, matrix decompositions (SVD, Eigendecomposition), tensor algebra, and linear transformations powering deep learning.
* [**Statistics & Probability**](Statistics-and-Probability.md) — Bayesian inference, probability distributions, hypothesis testing, maximum likelihood estimation (MLE), and probabilistic programming.
* [**Optimization**](Optimization.md) — Convex optimization, gradient descent variants, stochastic optimization, duality, and automated hyperparameter tuning.
* [**Automatic Differentiation**](Automatic-Differentiation.md) — Computational graphs, forward and reverse-mode autodiff, vector-Jacobian products (VJPs), and engine internals in PyTorch and JAX.
* [**Data Visualization**](Data-Visualization.md) — Exploratory data analysis, interactive dashboarding, high-dimensional latent space projection, and visual model debugging.

## 🧭 Recommended Study Sequence

1. **Linear Algebra & Vector Spaces**: Master dot products, matrix multiplications, projection, eigenvalues, and SVD.
2. **Multivariable Calculus & Autodiff**: Gradients, Jacobians, Hessians, the chain rule, and computational graphs.
3. **Probability & Statistics**: Random variables, Bayes' theorem, expectation, variance, information theory, and MLE.
4. **Numerical Optimization**: Convexity, gradient descent, momentum, Adam, and constrained optimization.
5. **Exploratory Visualization**: Plotting loss landscapes, embeddings, distributions, and interactive ML demos.

## 🗝️ Foundational Prerequisites

- High-school to undergraduate calculus (limits, derivatives, partial derivatives, integrals).
- Proficiency in Python and basic scientific programming.
- Familiarity with array indexing and broadcasting conventions.
