# Mathematics & Linear Algebra

> Linear algebra and multivariable calculus are the primary languages of artificial intelligence, describing high-dimensional data transformations, projection, and neural representation spaces.

## 🎯 What to Learn

- Vector spaces, subspaces, linear independence, basis, and dimension
- Matrix operations, determinant, rank, trace, and tensor contractions
- Eigenvalues, eigenvectors, and Spectral Decomposition
- Singular Value Decomposition (SVD) and Principal Component Analysis (PCA)
- Matrix norms (Frobenius, L1, L2) and condition numbers
- Multivariable calculus: gradients, directional derivatives, Hessians, and Jacobians
- Tensor shapes, Einstein summation (einsum), and memory layout (row-major vs column-major)

## 🧭 Recommended Learning Path

1. Master geometric vector operations: dot products, projections, linear combinations, and span.
2. Understand matrix multiplication as a linear transformation of basis vectors.
3. Study determinant, inverse, rank, nullspace, and systems of linear equations.
4. Dive into Eigendecomposition and SVD; understand why SVD applies to non-square matrices.
5. Implement einsum operations and tensor contractions for multi-head attention mechanisms.
