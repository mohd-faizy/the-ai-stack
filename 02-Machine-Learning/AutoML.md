# AutoML

> Automated Machine Learning (AutoML) automates the end-to-end process of applying machine learning, including data preprocessing, model selection, hyperparameter optimization, and ensembling.

## 🎯 What to Learn

- Automated feature preprocessing, imputation, and categorical transformation
- Combined Algorithm Selection and Hyperparameter optimization (CASH)
- Multi-layer stacking and ensembling techniques (AutoGluon stack ensembling)
- Bayesian optimization with warm-starting and meta-learning
- Early stopping and multi-fidelity optimization (Successive Halving, Hyperband)
- Trade-offs between search space breadth, compute budget, and overfitting

## 🧭 Recommended Learning Path

1. Understand the CASH formulation and why manual grid search fails in high-dimensional spaces.
2. Explore Bayesian Optimization and Multi-Fidelity optimization algorithms (Hyperband, BOHB).
3. Learn stacking, blending, and out-of-fold feature generation mechanisms.
4. Deploy AutoGluon and FLAML across diverse datasets and benchmark resource consumption.
5. Analyze the interpretability and post-hoc explanations of AutoML ensemble predictions.
