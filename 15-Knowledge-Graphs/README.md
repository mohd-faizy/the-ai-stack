# 15 - Knowledge Graphs

> Graph databases, Graph Neural Networks (GNNs), entity-relationship extraction, and structured ontological knowledge representation.

## 🌐 Overview & Architectural Importance

Knowledge Graphs represent information as interconnected networks of entities (nodes) and explicit semantic relationships (edges). In contrast to opaque neural vector representations, knowledge graphs provide deterministic, explainable, and multi-hop traversable reasoning—enabling fraud detection, biomedical discovery, and grounded enterprise AI systems.

## 📑 Curated Learning Guides

Explore in-depth roadmaps, authoritative textbooks, official documentation, courses, and implementations for each subtopic:

* [**Graph Databases**](Graph-Databases.md) — Neo4j, Memgraph, NebulaGraph, and graph query languages (Cypher, GQL) for low-latency network traversal.
* [**Graph Machine Learning**](Graph-Machine-Learning.md) — Graph Neural Networks (GNNs), PyTorch Geometric (PyG), DGL, and node/edge/graph classification.
* [**Knowledge Graph Construction**](Knowledge-Graph-Construction.md) — Automated extraction of entity-relation triples (SPO), ontology alignment, and entity resolution.

## 🧭 Recommended Study Sequence

1. **Graph Theory Basics**: Directed/undirected graphs, adjacency matrices, degree, centrality, and paths.
2. **Property Graphs & Querying**: Nodes, relationships, properties, and mastering the Cypher query language in Neo4j.
3. **Graph Neural Networks (GNNs)**: Message passing neural networks (MPNN), GCN, GAT, and GraphSAGE with PyTorch Geometric.
4. **Knowledge Graph Construction**: Extracting Subject-Predicate-Object (SPO) triples from unstructured text using LLMs.
5. **Enterprise Graph AI**: Connecting Knowledge Graphs to RAG architectures (Graph RAG) for explainable reasoning.

## 🗝️ Foundational Prerequisites

- Discrete mathematics: graph theory, vertices, edges, paths, and trees.
- Linear algebra: adjacency matrices and degree matrices.
- PyTorch tensor operations and message passing concepts.
