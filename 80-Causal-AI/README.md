# 80 - Causal AI

> Causal AI moves beyond statistical correlation to discover and quantify cause-and-effect relationships. By incorporating Pearl's Structural Causal Models (SCMs) and Rubin's Potential Outcomes framework, causal machine learning enables counterfactual reasoning ('what if?'), robust out-of-distribution generalization, and sound policy intervention design.
