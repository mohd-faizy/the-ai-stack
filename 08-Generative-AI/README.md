# 08 - Generative AI

> Generative foundation architectures spanning diffusion models, flow matching, Diffusion Transformers (DiT), video, audio, 3D, and full-stack generative applications.

## 🌐 Overview & Architectural Importance

Generative AI marks the transition of artificial intelligence from analytical classification to creative synthesis. Powered by latent diffusion, flow matching, and transformer backbones, generative systems produce photorealistic imagery, cinematic video, expressive voice, and interactive user interfaces from natural language descriptions.

## 🧭 Recommended Study Sequence

1. **Generative Foundations**: VAEs, Latent Spaces, and the transition from GANs to Diffusion.
2. **Diffusion Mechanics**: Forward noise process (DDPM), reverse denoising, score matching, and Rectified Flow.
3. **Conditioning Systems**: CLIP text encoders, cross-attention mechanisms, ControlNet, and IP-Adapter.
4. **Diffusion Transformers**: Scalable DiT architectures powering FLUX.1 and Sora-class models.
5. **Deployment & Tooling**: ComfyUI node graphs, Diffusers pipelines, TensorRT optimization, and full-stack interfaces.

## 🗝️ Foundational Prerequisites

- Multivariable calculus and probability distributions (Gaussian noise, Langevin dynamics).
- Transformer cross-attention mechanics and Vision Transformers (ViT).
- PyTorch tensor manipulations and GPU memory profiling.
