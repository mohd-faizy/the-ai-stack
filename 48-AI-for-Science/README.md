# 48 - AI for Science

> Applying deep learning, geometric neural networks, physics-informed architectures, and foundation models to fundamental scientific discovery across biology, chemistry, materials, and physics.

## 🌐 Overview & Architectural Importance

AI for Science represents one of humanity's greatest scientific leaps. Rather than relying solely on brute-force lab experiments or computationally intractable quantum simulations, models like AlphaFold, RFdiffusion, and GraphCast solve previously impossible 50-year-old scientific grand challenges in seconds.

## 📑 Curated Learning Guides

Explore in-depth roadmaps, authoritative textbooks, official documentation, courses, and implementations for each subtopic:

* [**Biology & Protein Science**](Biology-and-Protein-Science.md) — ESM, OpenFold, RFdiffusion, ColabFold, BioNeMo, and generative de novo protein design.
* [**Chemistry & Materials**](Chemistry-and-Materials.md) — RDKit, DeepChem, Open Catalyst Project, SchNet, e3nn, and equivariant graph neural networks.
* [**Drug Discovery**](Drug-Discovery.md) — DiffDock, TorchDrug, MoleculeNet, REINVENT, molecular docking, and ADMET prediction.
* [**Physics & Scientific Computing**](Physics-and-Scientific-Computing.md) — PINNs (DeepXDE), Fourier Neural Operators (FNO), NVIDIA Modulus, and JAX-MD.
* [**Climate & Weather**](Climate-and-Weather.md) — GraphCast, ClimaX, FourCastNet, Pangu-Weather, and global numerical weather prediction.

## 🧭 Recommended Study Sequence

1. **Geometric Deep Learning**: E(3) and SE(3) equivariance in 3D molecular structures.
2. **Protein Structure Prediction**: Evolutionary Scale Modeling (ESM-2/3) and AlphaFold/OpenFold evoformers.
3. **Generative Molecular Design**: Diffusion models for de novo protein and small-molecule design (RFdiffusion, DiffDock).
4. **Physics-Informed Neural Networks**: Encoding partial differential equations (Navier-Stokes) into loss functions.
5. **Global Atmospheric Modeling**: Graph neural networks on icosahedral meshes for 10-day weather forecasts.

## 🗝️ Foundational Prerequisites

- Vector calculus, differential equations, and linear algebra.
- Basic biochemistry (amino acids, proteins, SMILES strings) and physics principles.
- PyTorch Geometric, JAX, and 3D tensor manipulation.
