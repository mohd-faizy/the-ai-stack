# Legal NLP

> Specialized language models, citation extractors, and statutory reasoning systems trained on judicial rulings, court opinions, and legislative statutes.

## 🎯 What to Learn

- Legal language characteristics: Latin phrases, archaic terminology, hierarchical numbering, and dense cross-references
- Legal-BERT: BERT models pre-trained on diverse English legal text (legislation, court cases, contracts)
- SaulLM (Equall AI): First dedicated open-source 7B foundation model engineered specifically for legal practice and reasoning
- BlackStone: spaCy-based pipeline and models for processing unstructured legal text and citations
- Legal Benchmarks: LegalBench (162 collaborative legal reasoning tasks across 6 categories)
- Statutory interpretation, case precedent search, and citation network analysis

## 🧭 Recommended Learning Path

1. Understand the structural differences between legal drafting and common parlance.
2. Explore the LegalBench benchmark to understand what legal reasoning entails.
3. Use SaulLM or Legal-BERT for entity recognition (statutes, parties, judges) on court judgments.
4. Build a legal citation parser that extracts case references and resolves them to CourtListener APIs.
5. Implement a verified legal RAG system with strict quote verification.
