# Contract Analysis

> Automated contract review, clause classification, obligation extraction, and guided legal document assembly engines.

## 🎯 What to Learn

- Contract architecture: Recitals, covenants, representations, warranties, indemnification, and governing law clauses
- Clause extraction and classification: Extracting Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) terms and Master Service Agreements (MSAs)
- Docassemble: Open-source, interview-based document automation platform for assembling legal documents from rules
- Automated redlining: Comparing draft contracts against enterprise playbooks to flag non-standard clauses
- CUAD (Contract Understanding Atticus Dataset): Benchmark dataset of 510 contracts annotated with 41 clause types
- Detecting hidden risks: Unlimited liability, non-compete clauses, and unilateral termination triggers

## 🧭 Recommended Learning Path

1. Understand the key clauses in commercial agreements (Indemnity, Limitation of Liability, Termination).
2. Explore the CUAD dataset and its 41 legal clause labels.
3. Build a clause extraction pipeline using Hugging Face transformers.
4. Install Docassemble and build an interactive interview that generates a customized employment contract.
5. Implement automated redlining comparing contract text against a company playbook.
