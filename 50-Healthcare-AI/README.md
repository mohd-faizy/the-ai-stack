# 50 - Healthcare AI

> Clinical deep learning systems, 3D volumetric medical imaging segmentation, clinical record NLP, and biomedical multimodal foundation models.

## 🌐 Overview & Architectural Importance

Healthcare systems process petabytes of complex, heterogeneous data—CT/MRI volumetric scans, pathology whole-slide images, and unstructured electronic health records (EHR). Healthcare AI automates diagnostic workflows, detects early-stage tumors with superhuman sensitivity, and synthesizes clinical histories.

Explore in-depth roadmaps, authoritative textbooks, official documentation, courses, and implementations for each subtopic:

## 🧭 Recommended Study Sequence

1. **Volumetric Medical Imaging**: DICOM/NIfTI standards and 3D convolutional networks (nnU-Net).
2. **Clinical Language Processing**: Pre-training on PubMed and clinical notes (PubMedBERT, ClinicalBERT).
3. **Interactive Segmentation**: Zero-shot promptable segmentation for medical scans with MedSAM.
4. **Multimodal Clinical Reasoning**: Combining radiographic imaging with clinical history using LLaVA-Med.
5. **Clinical Deployment & Ethics**: HIPAA compliance, algorithmic bias, FDA approval pathways, and explainability.

## 🗝️ Foundational Prerequisites

- Medical imaging data standards: DICOM tags, NIfTI files, voxel spacing, and Hounsfield units.
- Computer vision architectures: 3D U-Net, Vision Transformers, and Dice loss coefficients.
- Clinical workflows and medical nomenclature (SNOMED CT, ICD-10, LOINC).
